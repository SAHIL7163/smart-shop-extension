# 🛍️ Smart Shopper – Find Cheaper & Better Products

Smart Shopper is a Chrome Extension that helps users find cheaper or better alternatives to products on Amazon.  
When browsing a product page, simply click the extension to get a list of AI-powered suggestions with prices, product images, links, and a quick comparison.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🔍 Automatic Product Detection – Works when you are on an Amazon product page  
- 🤖 AI-Powered Comparison – Suggests cheaper or higher-quality alternatives  
- 🖼️ Product Preview – Displays product images, prices, and direct links  
- ⚡ One-Click Access – Click the extension icon to see results instantly  
- 🌍 Supports Multiple Products – Get a list of similar items, not just one
